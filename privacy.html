SM Blockflare – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the SM Blockflare: Puzzle Blast mobile app (“Blockflare”, “the app”) handles when you play. The app is published by VSGames (“we”, “us”). In short: we do not run any servers, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your personal information ourselves.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your own phone, using the app’s local storage:

  • the player name you choose to type in (you can change it at any time);
  • your best score for each play mode.

This information is never sent to us or to any third party by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads may be shown as banners, full-screen ads between games, or optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a benefit (such as a hint or a shuffle).

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google under its own privacy policy; we do not receive this information. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google’s consent form. You can change your choice later and you can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not sell your data.

4. Children

Blockflare is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it.

6. Security

The app does not transmit your saved name or scores anywhere. Ads are delivered over secure connections by Google’s ad SDK.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
